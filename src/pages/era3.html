2048: Nowhere to Run

Short film · 2017Luke ScottDave Bautista as Sapper Morton6 minutes · 16 Sep 2017

Sapper Morton, an old Nexus-8 combat model, lives quietly as a farmer outside Los Angeles. One trip into the city ends his hiding.

—THE MARKET

Ella

In the city, Sapper stops to talk with a girl, Ella, and gives her a book he likes, The Power and the Glory. He sells the leeches he farms at the market for $3,000, $1,000 short of what he needs, and walks past a gang of thugs who try to provoke him.

Later the same gang turns on Ella and her mother. Sapper steps in and beats them with a soldier's strength, killing most of them. Shaken by what he has done, he leaves, dropping his identity papers. A bystander who saw him earlier calls the LAPD to report a rogue replicant. That call is what sends K to his farm at the start of 2049.

二〇四九
2049

Blade Runner 2049

Film · 2017Denis VilleneuveRyan Gosling · Harrison Ford · Ana de Armas · Sylvia Hoeks

Thirty years after the original, a new-model replicant Blade Runner finds proof of something that should be impossible: a replicant who gave birth.

ITHE TREE

A box under a dead tree

K, a Nexus-9 Blade Runner, retires Sapper Morton at his farm. Under a dead tree he finds a buried box holding the bones of a replicant woman who died after giving birth by caesarean section. His superior, Lt. Joshi, sees a world-breaking secret and orders every trace of it, including the child, destroyed. K, whose serial number is KD6-3.7, goes home to Joi, his holographic companion.

IITHE HORSE

A memory that might be real

At the Wallace Corporation, archives identify the bones as an experimental Nexus-7: Rachael, who vanished with Deckard thirty years earlier. Niander Wallace wants the child, the key to replicants who can breed, and sends his enforcer Luv to follow K. The date carved into the tree, 6.10.21, matches a childhood memory of K's: a wooden horse he hid in a furnace. Records show two children born that day with identical DNA, a boy and a girl, and the girl died young. At an orphanage in the wastelands he finds the horse in the furnace, exactly where he remembered hiding it.

The memory designer Dr. Ana Stelline tells him the memory is real, someone lived it. K begins to believe he's the child. He fails his baseline test badly. Joshi gives him 48 hours to pass it, or be retired himself. Luv kills Joshi. Joi arranges for a replicant, Mariette, to sync with her so that the two can be together. The next morning Mariette plants a tracker in K's jacket.

IIILAS VEGAS
TURNING POINT

Deckard

Traces of radiation on the horse lead K to the ruins of Las Vegas and Rick Deckard, hiding there for thirty years. Luv attacks, destroys Joi and takes Deckard to Wallace, who offers him a copy of Rachael in exchange for the child. Deckard refuses, and Luv kills the copy. Mariette's tracker lets Freysa's replicant resistance find and rescue him. Deckard had helped them scramble the birth records and then left the child with them so that nobody could trace her through him. K learns the truth: the child was a girl. He isn't special. His memory was hers.

IVSNOW
FINALE

The daughter

Freysa wants Deckard dead before he can lead Wallace to his daughter. K intercepts Luv's convoy at the sea wall instead, and drowns her in the surf. Badly wounded, he brings Deckard to Dr. Stelline's lab, the woman who makes memories and who is Deckard and Rachael's daughter. K gives Deckard the wooden horse outside and tells him to go in. Deckard goes in to meet her, while K lies down on the steps in the falling snow. The film never says K dies, but its writer Michael Green has confirmed it.

Blade Runner 2099

TV series · 8 episodesPrime Video · 25 November 2026Showrunner Silka Luisa

Fifty years after 2049, in a Los Angeles where humans have become second-class citizens to replicants, Olwen, played by Michelle Yeoh, is a veteran Blade Runner near the end of her life. She teams up with Cora, a mysterious new recruit played by Hunter Schafer, to investigate a conspiracy rooted in the city's darkest past.

Not yet released

All eight episodes arrive at once on Prime Video on 25 November 2026. Ridley Scott is an executive producer, and Jonathan van Tulleken directs the first two episodes. The cast also includes Dimitri Abold, Lewis Gribben, Sheila Atim and Tom Burke.
