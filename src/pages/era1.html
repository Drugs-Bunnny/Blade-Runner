How a burnt-out detective, a faked suicide and a sister in a borrowed body created the first Blade Runner.

Briefing

Before the unit existed

Blade Runner: Origins is set in 2009, ten years before the original film. Tyrell's Nexus 4 replicants have moved beyond off-world labour and combat and now work on Earth as servants for the wealthy. In secret, Tyrell is pushing toward a Nexus 5. There is no Blade Runner unit yet. The story explains why one had to be invented.

Published by Titan Comics from March 2021 to June 2022, written by K. Perkins and Mellow Brown with Mike Johnson, and drawn mainly by Fernando Dagnino with colours by Marco Lesko.

VOL. 1

Products

Issues #1–4. A noir murder mystery: a Tyrell "suicide" turns out to be a cover story laid over a cover story, and it ends with Cal blackmailed into hunting a replicant.

#01THE CASE

The case nobody wants solved

2007, the siege of Kalanthia. Soldiers Cal Moreaux and Avery are pinned down. Tyrell has supplied a crate to open "if all else fails." When Avery opens it, two combat replicants kill every enemy in the room. Cal's first contact with Tyrell's products is as weapons that saved his life.

Los Angeles, 2009. Cal now works cold cases for the LAPD and reads to his catatonic sister Nia in hospital. He is handed the apparent suicide of Dr. Lydia Kine, a Tyrell bioengineer found hanging in her office. He gets the case because he is discreet, and expendable. At Tyrell he questions Kine's assistant Effie Koropey until Ilora Stahl, Eldon Tyrell's chosen fixer, cuts in and hints he would be better used working for the company.

At her club, La Plume Sauvage, Divina warns him off a Tyrell job. Back home, Kine's brother Marcus insists his sister would never kill herself, and Cal pulls him clear of a crate tipped from the shadows. Then Effie calls: on the night Lydia died, a Nexus 5 prototype escaped.

#02THE SET-UP

The set-up

At the morgue, Kine's broken neck doesn't match a hanging. Sergeant Gutman is furious that Cal won't close the case. Stahl tells Effie that Kine wiped her project files before she died. When Cal gets back to his desk, Marcus is gone, taken by people nobody will name. Marcus had said another officer sent him to Cal. Someone is being lined up to take the fall.

Marcus is in Stahl's hovercar when a replicant, taken to be the escaped Nexus 5, hurls itself into it and brings it down in flames. At Nemo's Diner, Effie tells Cal that the Nexus 5 was given need, curiosity and hope. It wants more. Cal asks the question the series turns on: why make replicants look human at all?

#03THE REUNION

The reunion

Stahl walks away from a crash no human should survive, and her assistant Osha puts her on a call with Eldon Tyrell. Marcus is alive too, rescued by Asa, a replicant who says he is Lydia. At Cal's apartment, Effie hears him talk in his sleep about a troubling dream. They argue and split up: Effie goes for files that incriminate Tyrell, Cal goes to Lydia's apartment.

At a pierogi stand, Asa tries to win Marcus over with a shared childhood memory. Marcus watches the replicant cook strike back at an abusive owner and decides Asa is only a simulation of his sister. The issue ends with two traps closing. Stahl is waiting in Effie's apartment. Cal sets off the sprinklers at Lydia's, flushes out an ambush, and then Asa walks in. Cal raises his gun.

#04THE FIRST HUNT
TURNING POINT

The first hunt

Cal chases Asa across the rooftops and down rain-lashed streets, through a fall of several storeys into a glass enclosure, and into a museum. Effie follows, desperate to talk. There, Stahl shoots Effie dead. She gives Cal one week to eliminate the Nexus 5, or the slums burn and his sister pays. The first Blade Runner is made by blackmail.

Cal lets Asa say goodbye to Effie, and confirms the truth: Asa is Lydia, and Asa is not the Nexus 5. They hide Marcus with Divina. Asa warns that a second rogue replicant tried to kill Marcus. Then Cal tells Gutman, straight-faced, that it was suicide after all.

スクラップ
VOL. 2

Scrap

Issues #5–8. The story drops from Tyrell's towers to the slums where Cal grew up. Replicants start waking up, and the Nexus 5 turns out to carry the mind of the sister he has been visiting in hospital.

#05WHY?

Why?

Sick of the corruption tying the LAPD to Tyrell, Cal quits the force, but Stahl's deadline still binds him. With Asa as his partner, he checks on Arnault, a Nexus 4 servant stuck in a loop, repeating one word: "Why?" Asa crashes through a window to stop him attacking his owners.

At La Plume Sauvage, Asa finds nothing mechanically wrong. Divina suggests an existential crisis, which Nexus 4s supposedly can't have. Arnault dies. Stahl ambushes Cal at Nia's bedside and he sends her away. A slum contact, Lindon, points him to the All Caps bar and his childhood friend Desiree, where the doors lock behind him.

#06THE COAT
TURNING POINT

The coat

Carlisle's crew believe Cal sold them out to Tyrell, whom they blame for the disappearance of Carlisle's brother Eason. As a boy, Cal lined his coat with metal plates to protect Nia and Desiree in fights. He uses that coat as armour and a weapon to break free. The Blade Runner's trenchcoat began as slum armour.

Asa now suspects the killer is not the Nexus 5 but a Nexus 4 that has woken up, as Arnault did. At Lydia's old apartment, something bursts from the air vents and throws Asa and Marcus out of a window. Both barely survive. Desiree trusts Cal enough to lead him to the Nexus 5, arguing all the way about whether replicant lives matter. From a distance, Cal sees its face. It is his sister, Nia.

#07NIA

Nia

Flashbacks show that Nia's mind was moved into a Nexus 5 body when she was badly hurt. She was furious, then turned that anger into leading the awakened replicants. Asa's story deepens as well: Lydia lives in a male body, and Marcus cannot accept it. He tells Stahl everything and sides with Tyrell. Nia's crew raids a garage where replicants are rounded up and destroyed; her rule is to free them, and to punish only their abusers. The question is set: can Cal hunt his own sister, and does she hold the moral high ground she claims?

#08THE FIGHT

The fight

Cal learns that Kine was the one who transferred Nia, and turns on Asa. When Nia works out who Asa is, she beats Asa badly, and Asa kicks her down a flight of stairs. Meanwhile Filip, a murderous Nexus 4 that Nia awakened, goes for Cal. He tries to gouge out Cal's eyes and drives him through walls before Cal clotheslines him with a bundle of cables. Cal starts to strangle Asa and stops when Asa vows to be better than Lydia was. They find Filip torturing Nia, and Asa kills him. Then Stahl arrives at the slum market with Tyrell spinners and Marcus at her side, ready to burn it.

炎上
VOL. 3

Burning

Issues #9–12. A war, then a con. Stahl burns the slums, Nia plans one last move, and the Blade Runner unit is built on a staged killing.

#09SECTOR 6B

The Battle for Sector 6B

Stahl's week is up. She launches an all-out attack on Sector 6B with a private army of Nexus 3s, built strong and deliberately dim, ready to burn everything. Cal, Nia and Asa fight on the same side. Cal goes for Stahl alongside Isaac, Nia's lover, and promises him a drink afterwards. Giving Cal covering fire, Isaac is shot dead, and Stahl escapes in a spinner.

The neighbourhood holds, at a heavy cost. The alliance cracks. Marcus helps evacuate the slum, but Divina bans him from La Plume Sauvage for his part in the attack. The issue ends on a hard question: if replicants were made to serve, why give them feeling at all?

#10THE TEACHER

The teacher

2008. Nia teaches 5th grade and is put in charge of three Nexus 4s being trained to replace human teachers. She opposes the programme and erupts at Stahl. At Tyrell, Stahl calls Nia a poor candidate for a "burgeoning project." Lydia wants Effie's help persuading her. Effie refuses.

Isaac, one of the Nexus 4 trainees, brings Nia an apple and mentions he has read her thesis on empathic teaching. Then an explosion leaves her critically injured. Stahl has the ambulance intercepted, and Nia's mind is moved into a Nexus 5 in Kine's lab.

In 2009, at the ruins of La Plume Sauvage, Nia says "ego colligunt", "I will collect." They have brought Isaac's body with them. She proposes killing Stahl. Cal objects that it would make every replicant a target, but she leaves with Desiree, and he follows, asking Asa to stay with the survivors. Meanwhile Eldon Tyrell blames Stahl and orders her to repair the damage and make sure it never happens again.

#11THE BROADCAST
TURNING POINT

The broadcast

Desiree crashes a spinner onto Tyrell's roof to clear Nia's way in. Stahl is about to go live to announce rebuilding the slums with specialised replicants, and to admit that the Excel Program failed. Nia fights down from the roof while Cal crashes a car into the lobby and fights up. Nia reaches the studio first and holds Stahl at gunpoint on air.

Then the flashback reveals the truth. It was Nia's plan, and Cal agreed to it. The public needed a villain and a hero. Cal would chase her onto the broadcast and shoot her there, so viewers would see a replicant with a human face. If people believed "bad" replicants could be stopped, the "good" ones might be left alone.

She asks him to shoot her in the heart, not the head, so the viewers see her face and understand that replicants look just like them.

He does as she asks. Cal kills his sister on camera, and the city watches its first Blade Runner at work.

#12THE FIRST BLADE RUNNER
FINALE

The first Blade Runner

Two months after Nia's death, the LAPD blames Kine and Effie for the uprising. Citizens are killing replicants, and the media won't call it genocide. Asa and Divina shelter refugees and plan to move them to the forest where Lydia's family used to camp.

The LAPD Blade Runner Unit is founded with Cal as its first member. Stahl promises safer replicants, then tells Cal that rogue replicant calls will go through her office. The unit sits inside the LAPD, but Tyrell funds and oversees it. Cal vows to bring her down. Asa leaves Lydia's research diary for Marcus to find.

On a rooftop, Cal asks Asa to build a Voight-Kampff machine from Effie's research, to test Blade Runner applicants, and to let through people who might one day become too empathetic for the job. Before they part, he warns Asa that if they meet again, he will have to shoot him.

Analysis

Empathy is the line the story keeps drawing

  • Nia's thesis on empathic teaching#10
  • A servant replicant asks why#5
  • Cal's V-K test screens the hunters#12
  • The 2049 baseline test punishes feeling2049

Empathy is what wakes the replicants up. In Cal's hands, the test that measures it was meant to find hunters who might one day refuse to hunt.

Coerced, not chosen

Cal becomes a hunter through blackmail. The noble cop the public sees is a performance his sister scripted.

Identity and the body

Lydia in a male body and a teacher in a combat-grade prototype both ask whether a mind stays the same person in a new body.

Masks

Kine fakes her death, Stahl survives the impossible, Cal closes the case as suicide, and Nia plays the villain.

Designed obedience

Teaching models replace teachers, servants break down asking why, soldiers are built dim. Tyrell keeps designing obedience, and it keeps failing.

Icons explained

The trenchcoat is slum armour, the rooftop chase echoes the film, and the Voight-Kampff test is built from Effie's research.

Where Cal goes next

He becomes the first Blade Runner to retire, then a protector in the LA underworld. In 2039, Ash seeks him out to help her go after Niander Wallace.

Tokyo Nexus

ComicDie in Peace · 4 issues · Jul–Oct 2024Kianna Shore · Mariano Taibo

Two survivors of a failed mission in the war on Kalanthia run an unlicensed detective agency in Tokyo: Mead, a former marine, and Stix, a combat replicant. A missing-person case drags them into a war between the Yakuza, a Tyrell Blade Runner and Cheshire, a rival company building bootleg replicants.

#01THE FORGOTTEN

A sister goes missing

In a rough corner of Tokyo, a gang of wasureru, "Tokyo's forgotten", jumps Mead and slashes her leg. Stix turns up late and finishes the fight with thrown blades, then patches her up over takoyaki. She doesn't believe his excuse for being late.

A young woman, Sayo, hires them to find her missing sister Miyuki, a private investigator who vanished mid-case. Stix refuses the job and goes to volunteer at an underground food kitchen. Mead works it alone, gets little from Miyuki's fiancée, and realises Sayo's family runs on organised crime. By the end, an old acquaintance has warned Stix off, and Mead has found a room full of bodies.

#02RUMIKA

The Blade Runner from L.A.

Rumika, an American-born Blade Runner of Japanese descent whom Tyrell sends to Japan, arrives hunting a rogue replicant she believes is Stix. Word comes that Sayo's sister Miyuki has been found dead, apparently killed by a replicant. Mead points out that the killing doesn't match how Stix killed during the war. Stix asks a replicant shopkeeper about Cheshire's bootleg models, and thugs bundle the shopkeeper into a van.

At Miyuki's apartment they learn she was doing business with Cheshire. Then Sayo is shot dead with an arrow, and with her last breath she blames Stix. Mead sees Stix running from the scene and tells Rumika she saw nobody.

#03COSMOWORLD

The most human replicant

Rumours of a new combat model have Tokyo on the edge of a gang war: one Yakuza clan has been wiped out, and crews are attacking replicants. Mead reads the precision of Sayo's killing and insists it wasn't Stix. In a Cheshire lab, tests run on "Test Subject 23". Rumika learns that Cheshire has hired former Tyrell scientists, and she is ordered to arrest them or kill them. Asked about Stix, Mead calls him the most human replicant she has ever met.

Stix asks Mead to help free a replicant the Yakuza kidnapped, then walks out when he learns she's working with Rumika, collapsing a building behind him to lose the Blade Runner. At the Cosmoworld amusement park, Mead thinks she sees Stix dead and tears into a gang in a rage. He's alive. Rumika says she's no longer after him, and Stix warns that Blade Runners will come for every replicant in the end.

#04AURORA
FINALE

The stolen program

Takumi, Sayo and Miyuki's brother, who went along with the family business, is shot in the head by the Yakuza boss Alura. She offers Stix a longer life in return for a job, and she knows his model is changing. Mead reluctantly backs him. A grenade attack hits the building where Rumika has cornered the Tyrell scientists.

Pulling Rumika from the rubble, Mead reveals how she and Stix left the military: they stole the data for the Aurora Program, which could mass-produce replicants bred for killing. Alura wants it. Mead plans to use Alura's reach to find whoever leaked the program, and lets Rumika go to destroy it.

To Lose Is to Win

ComicTokyo Nexus · 4 issues · May–Aug 2026Nancy A. Collins · Mariano Taibo

Mead and Stix are still hunting the officer who left them for dead on Kalanthia, a failed off-world mission. Now he knows they survived, a Yakuza matriarch wants her daughters' killer found, and a Blade Runner is closing in on the last of the Aurora technology.

How these summaries were written

Issue #1 is summarised from published reviews. For #2 to #4, only Titan's own issue descriptions are available so far, so those entries cover each issue's set-up rather than how it plays out. Titan spells the Blade Runner's name both Rumika and Rumiko; this archive uses Rumika, as in the first series.

#01THE TRAITOR

Left for dead

A flashback to Kalanthia. Mead, a Corporate Marine, and Stix fight side by side, though not everyone accepts a human befriending a replicant. With Sergeant Major Lancaster, Mead reaches a contested command bunker and is ambushed by enemy helicopters. Mead and Stix switch on the Aurora program: Stix leads the other combat replicants to protect the humans while Mead directs them, and they escape through a tunnel beneath the tree line. The battle still haunts her.

Tokyo, 2015. Mead wakes from nightmares of the war. Stix has his own worries. Combat models expire sooner than others, and his Yakuza boss Alura, whose organisation protects the ex-Tyrell scientists behind Cheshire, has promised to help him outlive his date. He has also been quietly helping the community that runaway military replicants have built underground.

Their old commanding officer, Uldren, learns the two of them are alive and sets out to kill them before they can expose him: the data he stole from Tyrell is what let Cheshire build bootleg replicants with Stix's kill signature. Rumika, the Blade Runner from L.A., is back. Officially she is still after the scientists, but she is secretly hunting Aurora, a neural link that lets a human steer replicants from a distance. She confronts Mead with evidence of a super-replicant assassin who fights exactly like Stix, and the partnership starts to strain.

#02MADAME NOMURA

The matriarch

Madame Nomura, the matriarch of a powerful Yakuza clan, hires Mead to find out who murdered her two daughters: Miyuki, whose disappearance began the first series, and Sayo, the client who hired Mead to find her.

Stix, meanwhile, has been recruited by Cheshire to guard three of its most valuable scientists, the people building its bootleg replicants. When Tyrell learns what Cheshire is planning, it sends Rumika to destroy the facility, which puts Stix directly in her path.

#03OMIYA

Duel on Shiwa Shrine Road

Rumika follows the trail of the Aurora technology that Mead and Stix took from Kalanthia. It leads her to Omiya, where, on snowy Shiwa Shrine Road, she faces the shrine's deadly guardian in a samurai duel to the death.

In Tokyo, Mead discovers what killed Miyuki Nomura, and what she finds threatens to expose a conspiracy at the heart of the ruling Yakuza family.

#04THE UNDERCITY
FINALE

The last Aurora Yoke

The finale moves between two times. In the past, on Kalanthia, Mead comes face to face with the man who betrayed her and Stix and left their comrades to die.

In the present, deep beneath Tokyo in the Undercity, the poor underground community where runaway replicants live, Stix confronts Rumika over the last remaining Aurora Yoke. Cheshire believes the device can help it build a new generation of deadly replicants.

2019

Blade Runner

Film · 1982Ridley ScottSet November 2019Theatrical 1982 · Director's Cut 1992 · Final Cut 2007

The original. A burnt-out ex-Blade Runner is forced back to hunt four Nexus-6 replicants who have come to Earth looking for more life.

ITHE ARRIVAL

Four come home

Six Nexus-6 replicants kill 23 people off-world, take a shuttle and come to Earth, where replicants are banned on pain of death. One of them (two in the Final Cut) dies in an electrical field trying to break into the Tyrell Corporation. Another, Leon, has got himself hired at Tyrell, and shoots Blade Runner Holden on the second question of a Voight-Kampff test.

Captain Bryant pulls Rick Deckard out of retirement, shows him the recording of Holden's test, and sends the origami-folding Gaff along with him. Four are loose in the city: the leader Roy Batty, Leon, Zhora and Pris. Nexus-6s are built with a four-year lifespan. At Tyrell, Deckard tests Eldon Tyrell's assistant Rachael. It takes over a hundred questions to show she's a replicant who doesn't know it, carrying implanted memories from Tyrell's niece, which Tyrell calls an "emotional cushion". Later she shows Deckard a family photograph to prove she is human. He tells her the memories are implants, and she leaves in tears.

IITHE HUNT

Snake scales and photographs

In Leon's hotel room Deckard finds photographs and an artificial snake scale. The scale and a photograph lead him to Zhora, working as a snake dancer at a strip club. He chases her through the crowded streets and retires her. Leon ambushes Deckard and nearly kills him until Rachael shoots Leon.

Rachael has run from Tyrell, and Bryant adds her to Deckard's list. In his apartment Deckard promises he won't go after her, and the two begin an uneasy, troubling relationship.

IIITHE MAKER

More life

Roy and Leon visit a replicant eye-making lab, where the terrified eye-maker, Hannibal Chew, points them to J.F. Sebastian, who works closely with Tyrell. Pris wins the trust of this lonely genetic designer, whose body ages too fast. Through him, Roy reaches Eldon Tyrell. Roy asks his maker for more life. Tyrell explains it can't be done, and praises him as the brightest thing he has made. Roy kisses Tyrell, then kills him. Sebastian is later reported dead.

IVTEARS IN RAIN
FINALE

The rooftop

In Sebastian's apartment in the derelict Bradbury Building, Pris ambushes Deckard, and he retires her just as Roy returns. Roy hunts him through the rooms, breaks his fingers, and chases him onto the roof, where Deckard ends up hanging over the street. With his time running out, Roy pulls him up and saves him. He speaks of the things he has seen that no human would believe, and dies in the rain.

Gaff tells Deckard it's too bad Rachael won't live, "but then again, who does?" At his apartment Deckard finds a small origami unicorn on the floor, a sign that Gaff was there and let her be. He leaves with Rachael, not knowing how long she has. The unicorn is why people argue about Deckard. The 1992 Director's Cut drops the voice-over and the studio's happy ending and restores a short unicorn dream, which fans read as a hint that he is a replicant. The 2007 Final Cut, the only version over which Scott had full control, also has no voice-over and runs the dream at full length. Ridley Scott has said he saw him that way, while Harrison Ford and producer Michael Deeley wanted him human.

The game

PC game · 1997Westwood Studios · Virgin Interactive13 Nov 1997 · Enhanced Edition 23 Jun 2022

An original story running alongside the film. In November 2019, while Deckard hunts Roy Batty's group, a rookie Blade Runner named Ray McCoy works a case of his own out of the same precinct. Which suspects are replicants is shuffled at the start of every new game, and what McCoy does decides which of the thirteen endings you reach.

ITHE ANIMAL MURDERS

Runciter's

McCoy, a rookie under Lieutenant Edison Guzza while Captain Bryant is on sick leave, is sent to Runciter's pet shop. The store has been trashed and its animals slaughtered. The killers are replicants from Clovis's gang, punishing Runciter for harassing Lucy Devlin, a fourteen-year-old who works there and is Clovis's stepdaughter.

McCoy works it like a detective: collecting clues and photographs into his KIA database, enhancing pictures on the ESPER machine, and running Voight-Kampff tests. A veteran Blade Runner, Crystal Steele, talks down to the rookie, while Gaff drops the odd piece of advice.

IITHE SUSPECTS

Fifteen faces

The trail runs through Chinatown, Animoid Row, DNA Row, the Yukon Hotel and Early Q's club. Along the way McCoy meets Dektora, an exotic dancer; Gordo Frizz, a stand-up comedian; Izo, who supplies the gang with weapons; Sadik, Clovis's right-hand man and bomber; and Zuben, a cook at Howie Lee's restaurant.

Clovis and Zuben are always replicants. Lucy, Dektora, Gordo, Izo, Sadik and the twins Luther and Lance are shuffled at the start of each game, and at least one of Lucy, Dektora and Gordo is always a replicant. They behave differently depending on which they are, so the case plays out differently each time.

IIIFRAMED
TURNING POINT

The dirty lieutenant

Guzza has been secretly helping the replicants. He frames McCoy for the murder of a civilian, and McCoy has to go into hiding, with the department now hunting him.

He turns to Luther and Lance, twin genetic designers who once worked for Tyrell on replicant lifespans, and they give him evidence of Guzza's corruption. McCoy uses it to force Guzza to clear his name. At the handover in the sewers, replicants wound Guzza, and McCoy can either run or kill him.

IVTHIRTEEN ENDINGS
FINALE

Which side

By the end of Act III McCoy always faces a replicant: Dektora, Lucy or Gordo, depending on the game. Every choice up to here, including how McCoy handles each Voight-Kampff result and how he treats people, decides the ending.

The endings fall into three kinds. McCoy can stay a Blade Runner and hunt the replicants down, partnered with Crystal Steele if he saved her. He can side with the replicants: if he gathers four pieces of Nexus-6 DNA data, from Tyrell, Eisenduller, Sebastian, and Luther and Lance, Clovis believes he can restore their lifespans, and McCoy finds hints, never confirmed, that he may be a replicant himself. Or he can leave the city with neither side, alone or with Dektora or Lucy. Whether she is a replicant, and whether he holds all four pieces of DNA data, changes how that ending plays out.

Playing it today

Nightdive Studios released Blade Runner: Enhanced Edition in 2022 for PC, PlayStation 4, Xbox One and Switch. Its rebuilt graphics were poorly received, and the original game, running in ScummVM, was added to the Steam version days after launch. GOG had already re-released the original, also through ScummVM, in December 2019.

Blade Runner 2019

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Los Angeles · Off-World · Home Again, Home AgainJul 2019 – Nov 2020Michael Green · Mike Johnson · Andres Guinaldo

Aahna "Ash" Ashina, the LAPD's best Blade Runner, hides a failing spine inside a cybernetic brace. A missing-persons case in the same year as the film turns her against everything she was trained to do.

Vol. 1 · Los Angeles

#01ASH

The missing wife

Ash corners Benny, a replicant who killed five people. Offered the chance, he cuts out his own eyes and then his throat, which stuns her. Her boss, Wojciech, has a different job for her: Alexander Selwyn, head of the Canaan Corporation, wants his missing wife Isobel and four-year-old daughter Cleo found, quietly.

Ash finds their spinner south of the city viaduct with the chauffeur, Arkady, murdered inside. She goes home to recharge her back brace in a wheelchair. Across town, Isobel and Cleo walk to a meeting with a man called Malak. When three people jump them, Isobel takes all three down with frightening ease.

#02THE SKIN

Ask Selwyn

A back-alley surgeon called the Skin tells Ash that Isobel booked a "birthmark consultation" and talked about going home. The Skin, it turns out, is a Tyrell scientist officially dead for three years. Malak takes Isobel and Cleo into the Replicant Underground, to an ex-Tyrell engineer called the Lung.

Following the Skin, Ash finds Bellingham, a replicant surgically aged to look like an old man. He attacks her, then leaps from a window, telling her to ask Selwyn why he wants his daughter dead. Ash keeps that from Selwyn. Soon after, her spinner is shot out of the sky.

#03THE OFFER

Tyrell's version

Ash crawls out of the wreck before it explodes and wakes thirty hours later in hospital. The department now knows about her brace, Selwyn has had her taken off the case, and Harry Bryant's division has it. At Tyrell, Ms. Elo tells her the truth. Isobel is a replicant, a copy of Selwyn's dead wife that Eldon Tyrell gave him. Cleo carries a rare gene linked to a longer life. Tyrell wants Cleo back and offers to fix Ash's spine for good.

The smuggling gang known as the Bones takes Isobel and Cleo over the border. Among them is Bellingham, who admits they were the ones who shot Ash down. A boat pilot called the Heart carries the pair on to El Santuario, an island of free replicants. Ash tases her old partner Lelas before he can beat a suspect, then scares the suspect into naming a beach in Baja.

#04EL SANTUARIO
TURNING POINT

The trade

At El Santuario, the Underground shows Ash letters between Selwyn and Tyrell. Selwyn was going to hand Cleo over to Tyrell as payment for Isobel. Isobel ran to protect her, and meant to hide her there until she was old enough to fend for herself. Ash agrees to stand down.

Selwyn arrives with armed Tyrell men. In the firefight, Isobel stays behind to cover them while Ash escapes with Cleo. Back in Los Angeles, Ash decides to protect the girl rather than give her to the police or to Tyrell. Cleo says her mother told her about another safe place, and points at the sky.

Vol. 2 · Off-World

#05THE RABBIT

Seven years gone

In 2022 replicant production is banned, and the remaining Nexus-8s are left to live out their time serving their owners in the colonies. By 2026 Ash and Cleo are hiding on an off-world mining colony as Darja and Ian Kady, "mother and son". Ash is in a wheelchair. Cleo trades as "the Rabbit". Then rogue Nexus-8s storm the colony and free the replicant workers, and Cleo escapes in the chaos with a replicant friend, Padraic.

#06HYTHE

A warrant for Ash

Padraic brings Cleo to the Nexus-8 leader, Pellam, who wants transit ciphers. Cleo tells him the nearest are at Ramanuja station. Ash is pulled from the wreckage by a Blade Runner named Hythe, who gives her a Voight-Kampff test and knows exactly who she is: a fugitive wanted for the murder of Isobel Selwyn. Ash insists she's Darja Kady, then decides she's done running.

#07THE MAZE

Eyes without numbers

Hythe restores Ash's brace and badge in return for help finding Cleo. They trace the Nexus-8s to a surgeon who swapped out their serial-numbered eyes. Padraic refused. In the Maze, Ramanuja's worst borough, Pellam ambushes them and Hythe kills him. Cleo and Padraic ask the Hartawan, her old smuggling partner, to get them to the colony of Arcadia, and are told to change their looks before they board. Then Hythe introduces her real employer: Isobel Selwyn.

#08ARCADIA
TURNING POINT

The second Isobel

Isobel says she survived the firefight and has spent seven years searching. At the Arcadia departure gate, Ash spots Cleo. Hythe pulls a gun and reveals the trick. The Isobel Ash knew died. This one was built to lead them to the girl. Padraic tackles Hythe, and Hythe shoots him dead.

Then Isobel shoots Hythe. She carries the first Isobel's memories. She was made loyal to Selwyn, but her love for Cleo came with the memories and wins out. Ash leaves Cleo in her care to go on to Arcadia, and returns to Earth to finish things with Selwyn.

Vol. 3 · Home Again, Home Again

#09FOST

Back to the ruins

Ash wants Selwyn. Wojciech, now retired, tells her he has gone to ground. Her search leads to the ruins of the Tyrell headquarters, where squatters ambush her. She hides with Fost, a scientist from the Isobel project who still believes Tyrell is in business, and convinces him she's on the project too. Selwyn, warned, sends a new Hythe after her.

#10THE MAINFRAME

Arrested

Fost gets Ash into the Tyrell mainframe, where she finds the names linked to Project Isobel and a trail to Selwyn's hiding place. Tipped off by Selwyn, a squadron of LAPD spinners arrests her. On the way to prison the convoy is ambushed, and members of the replicant Resistance carry Ash off to a farm. Wojciech helped set up the rescue.

#11FREYSA

The Resistance

The Resistance leader, Freysa, starts repairing Ash's brace. A member called Pim has tipped off the new Hythe, who kills him at the farmhouse. A Resistance counter-attack gives Ash, Freysa and a boy, Ephraim, the chance to escape in Hythe's spinner. Freysa reveals Selwyn has been illegally building obedient Nexus-8s. Ash goes to Selwyn's old mansion in Santa Barbara and finds him waiting with Cleo's pet lion, now fully grown, and four more Hythes.

#12HOME AGAIN
FINALE

The family

Ash kills two Hythes and the lion, but Selwyn shuts down her brace remotely. Cleo and Isobel arrive. Selwyn admits he once meant Cleo for Tyrell's labs, and begs them to be a family again. Cleo hugs him, then goes to Ash, and Isobel snaps his neck. When one Hythe moves to kill Isobel, a defective Hythe shoots it. As payment she asks for the mansion and to be forgotten, and hands Ash the remote for her brace.

Cleo and Isobel head back to Arcadia. Ash stays on Earth to help the Nexus-8s, and the series closes with her eating noodles in the rain. Along the way she and Freysa become a couple, which is why the next series finds them together.
