東京
2015

Tokyo Nexus

ComicDie in Peace · 4 issues · 2024Kianna Shore · Mariano Taibo

Two survivors of the siege of Kalanthia run an unlicensed detective agency in Tokyo: Mead, a former marine, and Stix, a combat replicant. A missing-person case drags them into a war between the Yakuza, a Tyrell Blade Runner and Cheshire, a rival company building bootleg replicants.

#01THE FORGOTTEN

A sister goes missing

In a rough corner of Tokyo, a gang of wasureru, "Tokyo's forgotten", jumps Mead and slashes her leg. Stix turns up late and finishes the fight with thrown blades, then patches her up over takoyaki. She doesn't believe his excuse for being late.

A young woman, Sayo, hires them to find her missing sister Miyuki, a private investigator who vanished mid-case. Stix refuses the job and goes to volunteer at an underground food kitchen. Mead works it alone and realises Sayo's family runs on organised crime. By the end, an old acquaintance has warned Stix off, and Mead has found a room full of bodies.

#02RUMIKA

The Blade Runner from L.A.

Rumika, a Blade Runner sent from America, arrives hunting a rogue replicant she believes is Stix. She tells Mead and Sayo that Miyuki has been found dead, apparently killed by a replicant. Mead points out that the killing doesn't match how Stix killed during the war.

At Miyuki's apartment they learn she was doing business with Cheshire. Then Sayo is shot dead with an arrow, and with her last breath she blames Stix. Mead sees Stix running from the scene and tells Rumika she saw nobody.

#03COSMOWORLD

The most human replicant

Mead reads the precision of Sayo's killing and insists it wasn't Stix. In a Cheshire lab, tests run on "Test Subject 23". Rumika learns that Cheshire has hired former Tyrell scientists, and she is ordered to arrest them or kill them. Asked about Stix, Mead calls him the most human replicant she has ever met.

Stix asks Mead to help free a replicant the Yakuza kidnapped, then walks out when he learns she's working with Rumika, collapsing a building behind him to lose the Blade Runner. At the Cosmoworld amusement park, Mead thinks she sees Stix dead and tears into a gang in a rage. He's alive. Rumika says she's no longer after him, and Stix warns that Blade Runners will come for every replicant in the end.

#04AURORA
FINALE

The stolen program

Takumi, Sayo and Miyuki's brother, who went along with the family business, is shot in the head by the Yakuza boss Alura. She offers Stix a longer life in return for a job, and she knows his model is changing. Mead reluctantly backs him. A grenade attack hits the building where Rumika has cornered the Tyrell scientists.

Pulling Rumika from the rubble, Mead reveals how she and Stix left the military: they stole the data for the Aurora Program, which could mass-produce replicants bred for killing. Alura wants it. Mead plans to use Alura's reach to find whoever leaked the program, and lets Rumika go to destroy it.

Sequel · Tokyo Nexus: To Lose Is to Win · 4 issues · from May 2026

Nancy A. Collins and Mariano Taibo continue the story. Mead and Stix's old commanding officer, Uldren, learns they survived and wants them dead before they can expose him. He stole data from Tyrell that let Cheshire build bootleg replicants with the same kill signature as Stix, which explains the killings that framed him. The pair are also caught in the fallout of a Yakuza civil war, with Rumika sent to wipe out Cheshire and everyone tied to it.

ブレードランナー
2019

Blade Runner

Film · 1982Ridley ScottSet November 2019

The original. A burnt-out ex-Blade Runner is forced back to hunt four Nexus-6 replicants who have come to Earth looking for more life.

ITHE ARRIVAL

Four come home

A group of Nexus-6 replicants kill the crew of an off-world shuttle and come to Earth, where replicants are banned on pain of death. One dies trying to break into the Tyrell Corporation. Another, Leon, shoots Blade Runner Holden in the middle of a Voight-Kampff test.

Captain Bryant pulls Rick Deckard out of retirement, with the origami-folding Gaff tagging along. Four remain: the leader Roy Batty, Leon, Zhora and Pris. Nexus-6s are built with a four-year lifespan. At Tyrell, Deckard tests Eldon Tyrell's assistant Rachael. It takes over a hundred questions to show she's a replicant who doesn't know it, carrying implanted memories from Tyrell's niece.

IITHE HUNT

Snake scales and photographs

In Leon's hotel room Deckard finds photographs and an artificial snake scale. The scale leads him to Zhora, dancing at a club with a snake. He chases her through crowded streets and retires her through a shop window. Leon ambushes Deckard and nearly kills him until Rachael shoots Leon.

Rachael has learned what she is. In Deckard's apartment the two begin an uneasy, troubling relationship, and he has to ask himself whether she is on his list.

IIITHE MAKER

More life

Pris befriends J.F. Sebastian, a lonely genetic designer with a body that ages too fast. Through him, Roy reaches Eldon Tyrell. Roy asks his maker for more life. Tyrell explains it can't be done, and praises him as the brightest thing he has made. Roy kills Tyrell, and later Sebastian.

IVTEARS IN RAIN
FINALE

The rooftop

In the derelict Bradbury Building, Deckard retires Pris. Roy hunts him through the rooms, breaks his fingers, and chases him onto the roof, where Deckard ends up hanging over the street. With his time running out, Roy pulls him up and saves him. He speaks of the things he has seen that no human would believe, and dies in the rain.

Gaff lets Deckard know he could have killed Rachael and didn't. At Deckard's apartment, Deckard finds an origami unicorn on the floor and leaves with Rachael, not knowing how long she has.

二〇一九
2019

Blade Runner 2019

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Los Angeles · Off-World · Home Again, Home AgainMichael Green · Mike Johnson · Andres Guinaldo

Aahna "Ash" Ashina, the LAPD's best Blade Runner, hides a failing spine inside a cybernetic brace. A missing-persons case in the same year as the film turns her against everything she was trained to do.

Vol. 1 · Los Angeles

#01ASH

The missing wife

Ash corners Benny, a replicant who killed five people. Offered the chance, he cuts out his own eyes and then his throat, which stuns her. Her captain, Wojciech, has a different job for her: the tycoon Alexander Selwyn wants his missing wife Isobel and daughter Cleo found, quietly.

Ash finds their spinner under the city viaduct with the chauffeur, Arkady, murdered inside. She goes home to recharge her back brace in a wheelchair. Across town, Isobel and Cleo walk to a meeting with a man called Malak. When three people jump them, Isobel takes all three down with frightening ease.

#02THE SKIN

Ask Selwyn

A back-alley surgeon called the Skin tells Ash that Isobel booked a "birthmark consultation" and talked about going home. Malak takes Isobel and Cleo into the Replicant Underground, to an ex-Tyrell engineer called the Lung.

Following the Skin, Ash finds Bellingham, a replicant surgically aged to look like an old man. He attacks her, then leaps from a window, telling her to ask Selwyn why he wants his daughter dead. Ash keeps that from Selwyn. Soon after, her spinner is shot out of the sky.

#03THE OFFER

Tyrell's version

Ash crawls out of the wreck before it explodes and wakes thirty hours later in hospital. The department now knows about her brace, Selwyn has had her taken off the case, and Harry Bryant's division has it. At Tyrell, Ms. Elo tells her the truth. Isobel is a replicant, a copy of Selwyn's dead wife that Eldon Tyrell gave him. Cleo carries a rare gene linked to a longer life. Tyrell wants Cleo back and offers to fix Ash's spine for good.

The smuggler known as the Bones takes Isobel and Cleo over the border to a boatman, the Heart, and on to El Santuario, an island of free replicants. Ash tases her old partner Lelas before he can beat a suspect, then scares the suspect into naming a beach in Baja.

#04EL SANTUARIO
TURNING POINT

The trade

At El Santuario, the Underground shows Ash letters between Selwyn and Tyrell. Selwyn was going to hand Cleo over to Tyrell as payment for Isobel. Isobel ran to protect her. Ash agrees to stand down.

Selwyn arrives with armed Tyrell men. In the firefight, Isobel stays behind to cover them while Ash escapes with Cleo. Back in Los Angeles, Ash decides to protect the girl rather than give her to the police or to Tyrell. Cleo says her mother told her about another safe place, and points at the sky.

Vol. 2 · Off-World

#05THE RABBIT

Seven years gone

In 2022 replicant production is banned, and the remaining Nexus-8s are left to live out their time serving their owners in the colonies. By 2026 Ash and Cleo are hiding on an off-world mining colony as Darja and Ian Kady, "mother and son". Ash is in a wheelchair. Cleo trades as "the Rabbit". Then rogue Nexus-8s storm the colony and free the replicant workers, and Cleo escapes in the chaos with a replicant friend, Padraic.

#06HYTHE

A warrant for Ash

Padraic brings Cleo to the Nexus-8 leader, Pellam, who wants transit ciphers. Cleo tells him the nearest are at Ramanuja station. Ash is pulled from the wreckage by a Blade Runner named Hythe, who gives her a Voight-Kampff test and knows exactly who she is: a fugitive wanted for the murder of Isobel Selwyn. Ash insists she's Darja Kady, then decides she's done running.

#07THE MAZE

Eyes without numbers

Hythe restores Ash's brace and badge in return for help finding Cleo. They trace the Nexus-8s to a surgeon who swapped out their serial-numbered eyes. Padraic refused. In the Maze, Ramanuja's worst borough, Pellam ambushes them and Hythe kills him. Cleo and Padraic ask a fixer, the Hartawan, to get them to the colony of Arcadia. Then Hythe introduces her real employer: Isobel Selwyn.

#08ARCADIA
TURNING POINT

The second Isobel

Isobel says she survived the firefight and has spent seven years searching. At the Arcadia departure gate, Ash spots Cleo. Hythe pulls a gun and reveals the trick. The Isobel Ash knew died. This one was built to lead them to the girl. Padraic tackles Hythe, and Hythe shoots him dead.

Then Isobel shoots Hythe. She carries the first Isobel's memories, and her love for Cleo came with them. Ash leaves Cleo in her care to go on to Arcadia, and returns to Earth to finish things with Selwyn.

Vol. 3 · Home Again, Home Again

#09FOST

Back to the ruins

Ash wants Selwyn. Wojciech, now retired, tells her he has gone to ground. Her search leads to the ruins of the Tyrell headquarters, where squatters ambush her. She hides with Fost, a scientist from the Isobel project who still believes Tyrell is in business, and convinces him she's on the project too. Selwyn, warned, sends a new Hythe after her.

#10THE MAINFRAME

Arrested

Fost gets Ash into the Tyrell mainframe, where she finds the names linked to Project Isobel and a trail to Selwyn's hiding place. Tipped off by Selwyn, a squadron of LAPD spinners arrests her. On the way to prison the convoy is ambushed, and members of the replicant Resistance carry Ash off to a farm.

#11FREYSA

The Resistance

The Resistance leader, Freysa, starts repairing Ash's brace. A member called Pim has tipped off the new Hythe, who kills him at the farmhouse. Ash, Freysa and a boy, Ephraim, escape in Hythe's spinner. Freysa reveals Selwyn has been illegally building Nexus-8s. Ash goes to Selwyn's old mansion and finds him waiting with Cleo's pet lion, now fully grown, and four more Hythes.

#12HOME AGAIN
FINALE

The family

Ash kills two Hythes and the lion, but Selwyn shuts down her brace remotely. Cleo and Isobel arrive. Selwyn begs them to be a family again, and Isobel snaps his neck. When one Hythe moves to kill her, a defective Hythe shoots it, and asks for the mansion and to be left alone as payment.

Cleo and Isobel head back to Arcadia. Ash stays on Earth to help the Nexus-8s, alongside Freysa.
