Briefing

Before the unit existed

Blade Runner: Origins is set in 2009, ten years before the original film. Tyrell's Nexus 4 replicants have moved beyond off-world labour and combat and now work on Earth as servants for the wealthy. In secret, Tyrell is pushing toward a Nexus 5. There is no Blade Runner unit yet. The story explains why one had to be invented.

Published by Titan Comics in 2021–2022, written by K. Perkins and Mellow Brown with Mike Johnson, and drawn mainly by Fernando Dagnino with colours by Marco Lesko.

VOL. 1

Products

Issues #1–4. A noir murder mystery: a Tyrell "suicide" turns out to be a cover story laid over a cover story, and it ends with Cal blackmailed into hunting a replicant.

#01THE CASE

The case nobody wants solved

2007, the siege of Kalanthia. Soldiers Cal Moreaux and Avery are pinned down. Tyrell has supplied a crate to open "if all else fails." When Avery opens it, two combat replicants kill every enemy in the room. Cal's first contact with Tyrell's products is as weapons that saved his life.

Los Angeles, 2009. Cal now works cold cases for the LAPD and reads to his catatonic sister Nia in hospital. He is handed the apparent suicide of Dr. Lydia Kine, a Tyrell bioengineer found hanging in her sealed lab. He gets the case because he is discreet, and expendable. At Tyrell he questions Kine's assistant Effie Koropey until Ilora Stahl, Eldon Tyrell's chosen fixer, cuts in and hints he would be better used working for the company.

Divina warns him off a Tyrell job. Back home, Kine's brother Marcus insists his sister would never kill herself, and Cal pulls him clear of a crate tipped from the shadows. Then Effie calls: on the night Lydia died, a Nexus 5 prototype escaped.

#02THE SET-UP

The set-up

At the morgue, Kine's wounds don't match a suicide. Sergeant Gutman is furious that Cal won't close the case. When Cal gets back to his desk, Marcus is gone, taken by people nobody will name. Marcus had said another officer sent him to Cal. Cal is being lined up to take the fall.

Marcus is in Stahl's hovercar when the escaped Nexus 5 hurls itself into it and brings it down in flames. At Nemo's Diner, Effie tells Cal that the Nexus 5 was given need, curiosity and hope. It wants more. Cal asks the question the series turns on: why make replicants look human at all?

#03THE REUNION

The reunion

Stahl walks away from a crash no human should survive. Marcus is alive too, rescued by Asa, a replicant who says he is Lydia. At Cal's apartment, Effie hears him dream of a childhood moment on a rooftop with his sister. They argue and split up: Effie goes for files that incriminate Tyrell, Cal goes to Lydia's apartment.

Marcus watches a replicant cook strike back at an abusive owner and decides Asa is only a simulation of his sister. The issue ends with two traps closing. Stahl is waiting in Effie's apartment. Cal sets off the sprinklers at Lydia's, flushes out an ambush, and then Asa walks in. Cal raises his gun.

#04THE FIRST HUNT
TURNING POINT

The first hunt

Cal chases Asa across the rooftops and down rain-lashed streets, leaping car roofs, into a museum. Effie follows, desperate to talk. There, Stahl shoots Effie dead. She gives Cal one week to eliminate the Nexus 5, or the slums burn and his sister pays. The first Blade Runner is made by blackmail.

Cal lets Asa say goodbye to Effie, and confirms the truth: Asa is Lydia, and Asa is not the Nexus 5. They hide Marcus with Divina. Then Cal tells Gutman, straight-faced, that it was suicide after all.

スクラップ
VOL. 2

Scrap

Issues #5–8. The story drops from Tyrell's towers to the slums where Cal grew up. Replicants start waking up, and the Nexus 5 turns out to be the person he has been visiting in hospital.

#05WHY?

Why?

Sick of the corruption tying the LAPD to Tyrell, Cal quits the force, but Stahl's deadline still binds him. With Asa as his partner, he checks on Arnault, a Nexus 4 servant stuck in a loop, repeating one word: "Why?" Asa crashes through a window to stop him attacking his owners.

At La Plume Sauvage, Asa finds nothing mechanically wrong. Divina suggests an existential crisis, which Nexus 4s supposedly can't have. Arnault dies. Stahl ambushes Cal at Nia's bedside and he sends her away. A slum contact points him to the All Caps bar and his childhood friend Desiree, where the doors lock behind him.

#06THE COAT
TURNING POINT

The coat

Carlisle's crew believe Cal sold them out to Tyrell, whom they blame for the disappearance of Carlisle's brother Eason. As a boy, Cal lined his coat with metal plates to protect Nia and Desiree in fights. When they hand him back his coat, he uses it as armour and a weapon. The Blade Runner's trenchcoat began as slum armour.

Desiree trusts him enough to lead him to the Nexus 5, arguing all the way about whether replicant lives matter. From a distance, Cal sees its face. It is his sister, Nia.

#07NIA

Nia

Flashbacks show that Nia's mind was moved into a Nexus 5 body when she was badly hurt. She was furious, then turned that anger into leading the awakened replicants. Asa's story deepens as well: Lydia lives in a male body, and Marcus cannot accept it. The question is set: can Cal hunt his own sister, and does she hold the moral high ground she claims?

#08THE FIGHT

The fight

Cal learns that Kine was the one who transferred Nia, and turns on Asa. Filip, a violent replicant in Nia's group, attacks. He tries to gouge out Cal's eyes and drives him through walls before Cal clotheslines him with a bundle of cables. Cal starts to strangle Asa and stops when Asa vows to be better than Lydia was. Asa kills Filip. Stahl now has the three of them in her sights.

炎上
VOL. 3

Burning

Issues #9–12. A war, then a con. Stahl burns the slums, Nia plans one last move, and the Blade Runner unit is built on a staged killing.

#09SECTOR 6B

The Battle for Sector 6B

Stahl's week is up. She launches an all-out attack on Sector 6B with a private army of Nexus 3s, built strong and deliberately dim, ready to burn everything. Cal, Nia and Asa fight on the same side. Cal goes for Stahl alongside Isaac, Nia's lover, and promises him a drink afterwards. Isaac is shot dead, and Stahl escapes in a spinner.

The neighbourhood holds, at a heavy cost. The alliance cracks and Marcus is no longer welcome. The issue ends on a hard question: if replicants were made to serve, why give them feeling at all?

#10THE TEACHER

The teacher

2008. Nia teaches 5th grade and is put in charge of three Nexus 4s being trained to replace human teachers. She opposes the programme and erupts at Stahl. At Tyrell, Stahl calls Nia a poor candidate for a "burgeoning project." Lydia wants Effie's help persuading her. Effie refuses.

Isaac brings Nia a decorative apple and mentions he has read her thesis on empathic teaching. She invites him to lunch. Then an explosion leaves her critically injured. Stahl has the ambulance intercepted, and Nia's mind is moved into a Nexus 5 in Kine's lab.

In 2009, at the ruins of La Plume Sauvage, Nia says "ego colligunt", "I will collect." She proposes killing Stahl. Cal objects, but she leaves with Desiree, and he follows. Meanwhile Eldon Tyrell orders Stahl to stop this from ever happening in future models.

#11THE BROADCAST
TURNING POINT

The broadcast

Desiree crashes a spinner onto Tyrell's roof to clear Nia's way in. Stahl is about to go live to announce rebuilding the slums with specialised replicants. Nia fights down from the roof while Cal crashes a car into the lobby and fights up. Nia reaches the studio first and holds Stahl at gunpoint on air.

Then the flashback reveals the truth. It was Nia's plan, and Cal agreed to it. The public needed a villain and a hero. If people believed "bad" replicants could be stopped, the "good" ones might be left alone.

She asks him to shoot her in the heart, not the head, so the viewers see her face and understand that replicants look just like them.

He does as she asks. Cal kills his sister on camera, and the city watches its first Blade Runner at work.

#12THE FIRST BLADE RUNNER
FINALE

The first Blade Runner

Two months later, the LAPD blames Kine and Effie for the uprising. Citizens are killing replicants, and the media won't call it genocide. Asa and Divina shelter refugees and plan to move them to the forest where Lydia's family used to camp.

The LAPD Blade Runner Unit is founded with Cal as its first member. Stahl promises safer replicants, then tells Cal that rogue replicant calls will go through her office. He vows to bring her down.

On a rooftop, Cal asks Asa to build a Voight-Kampff machine from Effie's research, to test Blade Runner applicants, and to let through people who might one day become too empathetic for the job. Before they part, he warns Asa that if they meet again, he will have to shoot him.

Analysis

Empathy is the line the story keeps drawing

  • Nia's thesis on empathic teaching#10
  • A servant replicant asks why#5
  • Cal's V-K test screens the hunters#12
  • The 2049 baseline test punishes feeling2049

Empathy is what wakes the replicants up. In Cal's hands, the test that measures it was meant to find hunters who might one day refuse to hunt.

Coerced, not chosen

Cal becomes a hunter through blackmail. The noble cop the public sees is a performance his sister scripted.

Identity and the body

Lydia in a male body and a teacher in a combat-grade prototype both ask whether a mind stays the same person in a new body.

Masks

Kine fakes her death, Stahl survives the impossible, Cal closes the case as suicide, and Nia plays the villain.

Designed obedience

Teaching models replace teachers, servants break down asking why, soldiers are built dim. Tyrell keeps designing obedience, and it keeps failing.

Icons explained

The trenchcoat is slum armour, the rooftop chase echoes the film, and the Voight-Kampff test is built from Effie's research.

Where Cal goes next

He becomes the first Blade Runner to retire, then a protector in the LA underworld. In 2039, Ash seeks him out to help her go after Niander Wallace.
