Sapper Morton, an old Nexus-8 hiding as a protein farmer, comes into the city to sell his produce. When thugs go after a mother and her young daughter he has befriended, he steps in and kills them with a soldier's skill. Someone sees him do it. 二〇四九
2049

Blade Runner 2049

Film · 2017Denis VilleneuveRyan Gosling · Harrison Ford · Ana de Armas · Sylvia Hoeks

Thirty years after the original, a new-model replicant Blade Runner finds proof of something that should be impossible: a replicant who gave birth.

ITHE TREE

A box under a dead tree

K, a Nexus-9 Blade Runner, retires Sapper Morton at his farm. Under a dead tree he finds a buried box holding the bones of a replicant woman who died after giving birth by caesarean section. His superior, Lt. Joshi, sees a world-breaking secret and orders every trace of it, including the child, destroyed. K goes home to Joi, his holographic companion.

IITHE HORSE

A memory that might be real

At the Wallace Corporation, archives identify the bones as Rachael. Niander Wallace wants the child, the key to replicants who can breed, and sends his enforcer Luv to follow K. A date carved into the tree matches a childhood memory of K's: a wooden horse he hid in a furnace. At an orphanage in San Diego he finds the horse exactly where he remembered it.

The memory designer Dr. Ana Stelline tells him the memory is real, someone lived it. K begins to believe he's the child. He fails his baseline test badly, and Joshi gives him time to disappear before she reports him. Luv kills Joshi.

IIILAS VEGAS
TURNING POINT

Deckard

Traces of radiation on the horse lead K to the ruins of Las Vegas and Rick Deckard, hiding there for thirty years. Luv attacks, destroys Joi and takes Deckard to Wallace, who offers him a copy of Rachael. Deckard refuses. Rescued by Freysa's replicant resistance, K learns the truth: the child was a girl. He isn't special. His memory was hers.

IVSNOW
FINALE

The daughter

Freysa wants Deckard dead before he can lead Wallace to his daughter. K intercepts Luv's convoy at the sea wall instead, and drowns her in the surf. Badly wounded, he brings Deckard to Dr. Stelline's lab, the woman who makes memories and who is Deckard and Rachael's daughter. Deckard goes in to meet her, while K lies down on the steps in the falling snow.
