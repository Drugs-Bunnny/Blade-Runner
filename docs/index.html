2009

Blade Runner: Origins

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Products · Scrap · BurningK. Perkins · Mellow Brown · Fernando Dagnino

How a burnt-out detective, a faked suicide and a sister in a borrowed body created the first Blade Runner.

Briefing

Before the unit existed

Blade Runner: Origins is set in 2009, ten years before the original film. Tyrell's Nexus 4 replicants have moved beyond off-world labour and combat and now work on Earth as servants for the wealthy. In secret, Tyrell is pushing toward a Nexus 5. There is no Blade Runner unit yet. The story explains why one had to be invented.

Published by Titan Comics in 2021–2022, written by K. Perkins and Mellow Brown with Mike Johnson, and drawn mainly by Fernando Dagnino with colours by Marco Lesko.

製品
VOL. 1

Products

Issues #1–4. A noir murder mystery: a Tyrell "suicide" turns out to be a cover story laid over a cover story, and it ends with Cal blackmailed into hunting a replicant.

#01THE CASE

The case nobody wants solved

2007, the siege of Kalanthia. Soldiers Cal Moreaux and Avery are pinned down. Tyrell has supplied a crate to open "if all else fails." When Avery opens it, two combat replicants kill every enemy in the room. Cal's first contact with Tyrell's products is as weapons that saved his life.

Los Angeles, 2009. Cal now works cold cases for the LAPD and reads to his catatonic sister Nia in hospital. He is handed the apparent suicide of Dr. Lydia Kine, a Tyrell bioengineer found hanging in her sealed lab. He gets the case because he is discreet, and expendable. At Tyrell he questions Kine's assistant Effie Koropey until Ilora Stahl, Eldon Tyrell's chosen fixer, cuts in and hints he would be better used working for the company.

Divina warns him off a Tyrell job. Back home, Kine's brother Marcus insists his sister would never kill herself, and Cal pulls him clear of a crate tipped from the shadows. Then Effie calls: on the night Lydia died, a Nexus 5 prototype escaped.

#02THE SET-UP

The set-up

At the morgue, Kine's wounds don't match a suicide. Sergeant Gutman is furious that Cal won't close the case. When Cal gets back to his desk, Marcus is gone, taken by people nobody will name. Marcus had said another officer sent him to Cal. Cal is being lined up to take the fall.

Marcus is in Stahl's hovercar when the escaped Nexus 5 hurls itself into it and brings it down in flames. At Nemo's Diner, Effie tells Cal that the Nexus 5 was given need, curiosity and hope. It wants more. Cal asks the question the series turns on: why make replicants look human at all?

#03THE REUNION

The reunion

Stahl walks away from a crash no human should survive. Marcus is alive too, rescued by Asa, a replicant who says he is Lydia. At Cal's apartment, Effie hears him dream of a childhood moment on a rooftop with his sister. They argue and split up: Effie goes for files that incriminate Tyrell, Cal goes to Lydia's apartment.

Marcus watches a replicant cook strike back at an abusive owner and decides Asa is only a simulation of his sister. The issue ends with two traps closing. Stahl is waiting in Effie's apartment. Cal sets off the sprinklers at Lydia's, flushes out an ambush, and then Asa walks in. Cal raises his gun.

#04THE FIRST HUNT
TURNING POINT

The first hunt

Cal chases Asa across the rooftops and down rain-lashed streets, leaping car roofs, into a museum. Effie follows, desperate to talk. There, Stahl shoots Effie dead. She gives Cal one week to eliminate the Nexus 5, or the slums burn and his sister pays. The first Blade Runner is made by blackmail.

Cal lets Asa say goodbye to Effie, and confirms the truth: Asa is Lydia, and Asa is not the Nexus 5. They hide Marcus with Divina. Then Cal tells Gutman, straight-faced, that it was suicide after all.

スクラップ
VOL. 2

Scrap

Issues #5–8. The story drops from Tyrell's towers to the slums where Cal grew up. Replicants start waking up, and the Nexus 5 turns out to be the person he has been visiting in hospital.

#05WHY?

Why?

Sick of the corruption tying the LAPD to Tyrell, Cal quits the force, but Stahl's deadline still binds him. With Asa as his partner, he checks on Arnault, a Nexus 4 servant stuck in a loop, repeating one word: "Why?" Asa crashes through a window to stop him attacking his owners.

At La Plume Sauvage, Asa finds nothing mechanically wrong. Divina suggests an existential crisis, which Nexus 4s supposedly can't have. Arnault dies. Stahl ambushes Cal at Nia's bedside and he sends her away. A slum contact points him to the All Caps bar and his childhood friend Desiree, where the doors lock behind him.

#06THE COAT
TURNING POINT

The coat

Carlisle's crew believe Cal sold them out to Tyrell, whom they blame for the disappearance of Carlisle's brother Eason. As a boy, Cal lined his coat with metal plates to protect Nia and Desiree in fights. When they hand him back his coat, he uses it as armour and a weapon. The Blade Runner's trenchcoat began as slum armour.

Desiree trusts him enough to lead him to the Nexus 5, arguing all the way about whether replicant lives matter. From a distance, Cal sees its face. It is his sister, Nia.

#07NIA

Nia

Flashbacks show that Nia's mind was moved into a Nexus 5 body when she was badly hurt. She was furious, then turned that anger into leading the awakened replicants. Asa's story deepens as well: Lydia lives in a male body, and Marcus cannot accept it. The question is set: can Cal hunt his own sister, and does she hold the moral high ground she claims?

#08THE FIGHT

The fight

Cal learns that Kine was the one who transferred Nia, and turns on Asa. Filip, a violent replicant in Nia's group, attacks. He tries to gouge out Cal's eyes and drives him through walls before Cal clotheslines him with a bundle of cables. Cal starts to strangle Asa and stops when Asa vows to be better than Lydia was. Asa kills Filip. Stahl now has the three of them in her sights.

炎上
VOL. 3

Burning

Issues #9–12. A war, then a con. Stahl burns the slums, Nia plans one last move, and the Blade Runner unit is built on a staged killing.

#09SECTOR 6B

The Battle for Sector 6B

Stahl's week is up. She launches an all-out attack on Sector 6B with a private army of Nexus 3s, built strong and deliberately dim, ready to burn everything. Cal, Nia and Asa fight on the same side. Cal goes for Stahl alongside Isaac, Nia's lover, and promises him a drink afterwards. Isaac is shot dead, and Stahl escapes in a spinner.

The neighbourhood holds, at a heavy cost. The alliance cracks and Marcus is no longer welcome. The issue ends on a hard question: if replicants were made to serve, why give them feeling at all?

#10THE TEACHER

The teacher

2008. Nia teaches 5th grade and is put in charge of three Nexus 4s being trained to replace human teachers. She opposes the programme and erupts at Stahl. At Tyrell, Stahl calls Nia a poor candidate for a "burgeoning project." Lydia wants Effie's help persuading her. Effie refuses.

Isaac brings Nia a decorative apple and mentions he has read her thesis on empathic teaching. She invites him to lunch. Then an explosion leaves her critically injured. Stahl has the ambulance intercepted, and Nia's mind is moved into a Nexus 5 in Kine's lab.

In 2009, at the ruins of La Plume Sauvage, Nia says "ego colligunt", "I will collect." She proposes killing Stahl. Cal objects, but she leaves with Desiree, and he follows. Meanwhile Eldon Tyrell orders Stahl to stop this from ever happening in future models.

#11THE BROADCAST
TURNING POINT

The broadcast

Desiree crashes a spinner onto Tyrell's roof to clear Nia's way in. Stahl is about to go live to announce rebuilding the slums with specialised replicants. Nia fights down from the roof while Cal crashes a car into the lobby and fights up. Nia reaches the studio first and holds Stahl at gunpoint on air.

Then the flashback reveals the truth. It was Nia's plan, and Cal agreed to it. The public needed a villain and a hero. If people believed "bad" replicants could be stopped, the "good" ones might be left alone.

LIVE · TYRELL HQ
She asks him to shoot her in the heart, not the head, so the viewers see her face and understand that replicants look just like them.

He does as she asks. Cal kills his sister on camera, and the city watches its first Blade Runner at work.

#12THE FIRST BLADE RUNNER
FINALE

The first Blade Runner

Two months later, the LAPD blames Kine and Effie for the uprising. Citizens are killing replicants, and the media won't call it genocide. Asa and Divina shelter refugees and plan to move them to the forest where Lydia's family used to camp.

The LAPD Blade Runner Unit is founded with Cal as its first member. Stahl promises safer replicants, then tells Cal that rogue replicant calls will go through her office. He vows to bring her down.

On a rooftop, Cal asks Asa to build a Voight-Kampff machine from Effie's research, to test Blade Runner applicants, and to let through people who might one day become too empathetic for the job. Before they part, he warns Asa that if they meet again, he will have to shoot him.

Analysis

Empathy is the line the story keeps drawing

  • Nia's thesis on empathic teaching#10
  • A servant replicant asks why#5
  • Cal's V-K test screens the hunters#12
  • The 2049 baseline test punishes feeling2049

Empathy is what wakes the replicants up. In Cal's hands, the test that measures it was meant to find hunters who might one day refuse to hunt.

Coerced, not chosen

Cal becomes a hunter through blackmail. The noble cop the public sees is a performance his sister scripted.

Identity and the body

Lydia in a male body and a teacher in a combat-grade prototype both ask whether a mind stays the same person in a new body.

Masks

Kine fakes her death, Stahl survives the impossible, Cal closes the case as suicide, and Nia plays the villain.

Designed obedience

Teaching models replace teachers, servants break down asking why, soldiers are built dim. Tyrell keeps designing obedience, and it keeps failing.

Icons explained

The trenchcoat is slum armour, the rooftop chase echoes the film, and the Voight-Kampff test is built from Effie's research.

Where Cal goes next

He becomes the first Blade Runner to retire, then a protector in the LA underworld. In 2039, Ash seeks him out to help her go after Niander Wallace.

東京
2015

Tokyo Nexus

ComicDie in Peace · 4 issues · 2024Kianna Shore · Mariano Taibo

Two survivors of the siege of Kalanthia run an unlicensed detective agency in Tokyo: Mead, a former marine, and Stix, a combat replicant. A missing-person case drags them into a war between the Yakuza, a Tyrell Blade Runner and Cheshire, a rival company building bootleg replicants.

#01THE FORGOTTEN

A sister goes missing

In a rough corner of Tokyo, a gang of wasureru, "Tokyo's forgotten", jumps Mead and slashes her leg. Stix turns up late and finishes the fight with thrown blades, then patches her up over takoyaki. She doesn't believe his excuse for being late.

A young woman, Sayo, hires them to find her missing sister Miyuki, a private investigator who vanished mid-case. Stix refuses the job and goes to volunteer at an underground food kitchen. Mead works it alone and realises Sayo's family runs on organised crime. By the end, an old acquaintance has warned Stix off, and Mead has found a room full of bodies.

#02RUMIKA

The Blade Runner from L.A.

Rumika, a Blade Runner sent from America, arrives hunting a rogue replicant she believes is Stix. She tells Mead and Sayo that Miyuki has been found dead, apparently killed by a replicant. Mead points out that the killing doesn't match how Stix killed during the war.

At Miyuki's apartment they learn she was doing business with Cheshire. Then Sayo is shot dead with an arrow, and with her last breath she blames Stix. Mead sees Stix running from the scene and tells Rumika she saw nobody.

#03COSMOWORLD

The most human replicant

Mead reads the precision of Sayo's killing and insists it wasn't Stix. In a Cheshire lab, tests run on "Test Subject 23". Rumika learns that Cheshire has hired former Tyrell scientists, and she is ordered to arrest them or kill them. Asked about Stix, Mead calls him the most human replicant she has ever met.

Stix asks Mead to help free a replicant the Yakuza kidnapped, then walks out when he learns she's working with Rumika, collapsing a building behind him to lose the Blade Runner. At the Cosmoworld amusement park, Mead thinks she sees Stix dead and tears into a gang in a rage. He's alive. Rumika says she's no longer after him, and Stix warns that Blade Runners will come for every replicant in the end.

#04AURORA
FINALE

The stolen program

Takumi, Sayo and Miyuki's brother, who went along with the family business, is shot in the head by the Yakuza boss Alura. She offers Stix a longer life in return for a job, and she knows his model is changing. Mead reluctantly backs him. A grenade attack hits the building where Rumika has cornered the Tyrell scientists.

Pulling Rumika from the rubble, Mead reveals how she and Stix left the military: they stole the data for the Aurora Program, which could mass-produce replicants bred for killing. Alura wants it. Mead plans to use Alura's reach to find whoever leaked the program, and lets Rumika go to destroy it.

Sequel · Tokyo Nexus: To Lose Is to Win · 4 issues · from May 2026

Nancy A. Collins and Mariano Taibo continue the story. Mead and Stix's old commanding officer, Uldren, learns they survived and wants them dead before they can expose him. He stole data from Tyrell that let Cheshire build bootleg replicants with the same kill signature as Stix, which explains the killings that framed him. The pair are also caught in the fallout of a Yakuza civil war, with Rumika sent to wipe out Cheshire and everyone tied to it.

This series is still coming out, so detailed issue-by-issue summaries aren't available yet.

ブレードランナー
2019

Blade Runner

Film · 1982Ridley ScottSet November 2019

The original. A burnt-out ex-Blade Runner is forced back to hunt four Nexus-6 replicants who have come to Earth looking for more life.

ITHE ARRIVAL

Four come home

A group of Nexus-6 replicants kill the crew of an off-world shuttle and come to Earth, where replicants are banned on pain of death. One of them, Leon, has got himself hired at Tyrell, and shoots Blade Runner Holden on the second question of a Voight-Kampff test.

Captain Bryant pulls Rick Deckard out of retirement, with the origami-folding Gaff tagging along. Four are loose in the city: the leader Roy Batty, Leon, Zhora and Pris. Nexus-6s are built with a four-year lifespan. At Tyrell, Deckard tests Eldon Tyrell's assistant Rachael. It takes over a hundred questions to show she's a replicant who doesn't know it, carrying implanted memories from Tyrell's niece.

IITHE HUNT

Snake scales and photographs

In Leon's hotel room Deckard finds photographs and an artificial snake scale. The scale and a photograph lead him to Zhora, working as a snake dancer at a strip club. He chases her through the crowded streets and retires her. Leon ambushes Deckard and nearly kills him until Rachael shoots Leon.

Rachael has learned what she is. In Deckard's apartment the two begin an uneasy, troubling relationship, and he has to ask himself whether she is on his list.

IIITHE MAKER

More life

Pris wins the trust of J.F. Sebastian, a lonely genetic designer with a body that ages too fast. Through him, Roy reaches Eldon Tyrell. Roy asks his maker for more life. Tyrell explains it can't be done, and praises him as the brightest thing he has made. Roy kills Tyrell. Sebastian is later reported dead.

IVTEARS IN RAIN
FINALE

The rooftop

In the derelict Bradbury Building, Deckard retires Pris. Roy hunts him through the rooms, breaks his fingers, and chases him onto the roof, where Deckard ends up hanging over the street. With his time running out, Roy pulls him up and saves him. He speaks of the things he has seen that no human would believe, and dies in the rain.

Gaff tells Deckard it's too bad Rachael won't live, "but then again, who does?" At his apartment Deckard finds a small origami unicorn on the floor, a sign that Gaff was there and let her be. He leaves with Rachael, not knowing how long she has.

二〇一九
2019

Blade Runner 2019

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Los Angeles · Off-World · Home Again, Home AgainMichael Green · Mike Johnson · Andres Guinaldo

Aahna "Ash" Ashina, the LAPD's best Blade Runner, hides a failing spine inside a cybernetic brace. A missing-persons case in the same year as the film turns her against everything she was trained to do.

Vol. 1 · Los Angeles

#01ASH

The missing wife

Ash corners Benny, a replicant who killed five people. Offered the chance, he cuts out his own eyes and then his throat, which stuns her. Her captain, Wojciech, has a different job for her: the tycoon Alexander Selwyn wants his missing wife Isobel and daughter Cleo found, quietly.

Ash finds their spinner under the city viaduct with the chauffeur, Arkady, murdered inside. She goes home to recharge her back brace in a wheelchair. Across town, Isobel and Cleo walk to a meeting with a man called Malak. When three people jump them, Isobel takes all three down with frightening ease.

#02THE SKIN

Ask Selwyn

A back-alley surgeon called the Skin tells Ash that Isobel booked a "birthmark consultation" and talked about going home. Malak takes Isobel and Cleo into the Replicant Underground, to an ex-Tyrell engineer called the Lung.

Following the Skin, Ash finds Bellingham, a replicant surgically aged to look like an old man. He attacks her, then leaps from a window, telling her to ask Selwyn why he wants his daughter dead. Ash keeps that from Selwyn. Soon after, her spinner is shot out of the sky.

#03THE OFFER

Tyrell's version

Ash crawls out of the wreck before it explodes and wakes thirty hours later in hospital. The department now knows about her brace, Selwyn has had her taken off the case, and Harry Bryant's division has it. At Tyrell, Ms. Elo tells her the truth. Isobel is a replicant, a copy of Selwyn's dead wife that Eldon Tyrell gave him. Cleo carries a rare gene linked to a longer life. Tyrell wants Cleo back and offers to fix Ash's spine for good.

The smuggler known as the Bones takes Isobel and Cleo over the border to a boatman, the Heart, and on to El Santuario, an island of free replicants. Ash tases her old partner Lelas before he can beat a suspect, then scares the suspect into naming a beach in Baja.

#04EL SANTUARIO
TURNING POINT

The trade

At El Santuario, the Underground shows Ash letters between Selwyn and Tyrell. Selwyn was going to hand Cleo over to Tyrell as payment for Isobel. Isobel ran to protect her. Ash agrees to stand down.

Selwyn arrives with armed Tyrell men. In the firefight, Isobel stays behind to cover them while Ash escapes with Cleo. Back in Los Angeles, Ash decides to protect the girl rather than give her to the police or to Tyrell. Cleo says her mother told her about another safe place, and points at the sky.

Vol. 2 · Off-World

#05THE RABBIT

Seven years gone

In 2022 replicant production is banned, and the remaining Nexus-8s are left to live out their time serving their owners in the colonies. By 2026 Ash and Cleo are hiding on an off-world mining colony as Darja and Ian Kady, "mother and son". Ash is in a wheelchair. Cleo trades as "the Rabbit". Then rogue Nexus-8s storm the colony and free the replicant workers, and Cleo escapes in the chaos with a replicant friend, Padraic.

#06HYTHE

A warrant for Ash

Padraic brings Cleo to the Nexus-8 leader, Pellam, who wants transit ciphers. Cleo tells him the nearest are at Ramanuja station. Ash is pulled from the wreckage by a Blade Runner named Hythe, who gives her a Voight-Kampff test and knows exactly who she is: a fugitive wanted for the murder of Isobel Selwyn. Ash insists she's Darja Kady, then decides she's done running.

#07THE MAZE

Eyes without numbers

Hythe restores Ash's brace and badge in return for help finding Cleo. They trace the Nexus-8s to a surgeon who swapped out their serial-numbered eyes. Padraic refused. In the Maze, Ramanuja's worst borough, Pellam ambushes them and Hythe kills him. Cleo and Padraic ask a fixer, the Hartawan, to get them to the colony of Arcadia. Then Hythe introduces her real employer: Isobel Selwyn.

#08ARCADIA
TURNING POINT

The second Isobel

Isobel says she survived the firefight and has spent seven years searching. At the Arcadia departure gate, Ash spots Cleo. Hythe pulls a gun and reveals the trick. The Isobel Ash knew died. This one was built to lead them to the girl. Padraic tackles Hythe, and Hythe shoots him dead.

Then Isobel shoots Hythe. She carries the first Isobel's memories, and her love for Cleo came with them. Ash leaves Cleo in her care to go on to Arcadia, and returns to Earth to finish things with Selwyn.

Vol. 3 · Home Again, Home Again

#09FOST

Back to the ruins

Ash wants Selwyn. Wojciech, now retired, tells her he has gone to ground. Her search leads to the ruins of the Tyrell headquarters, where squatters ambush her. She hides with Fost, a scientist from the Isobel project who still believes Tyrell is in business, and convinces him she's on the project too. Selwyn, warned, sends a new Hythe after her.

#10THE MAINFRAME

Arrested

Fost gets Ash into the Tyrell mainframe, where she finds the names linked to Project Isobel and a trail to Selwyn's hiding place. Tipped off by Selwyn, a squadron of LAPD spinners arrests her. On the way to prison the convoy is ambushed, and members of the replicant Resistance carry Ash off to a farm.

#11FREYSA

The Resistance

The Resistance leader, Freysa, starts repairing Ash's brace. A member called Pim has tipped off the new Hythe, who kills him at the farmhouse. Ash, Freysa and a boy, Ephraim, escape in Hythe's spinner. Freysa reveals Selwyn has been illegally building Nexus-8s. Ash goes to Selwyn's old mansion and finds him waiting with Cleo's pet lion, now fully grown, and four more Hythes.

#12HOME AGAIN
FINALE

The family

Ash kills two Hythes and the lion, but Selwyn shuts down her brace remotely. Cleo and Isobel arrive. Selwyn begs them to be a family again, and Isobel snaps his neck. When one Hythe moves to kill her, a defective Hythe shoots it, and asks for the mansion and to be left alone as payment.

Cleo and Isobel head back to Arcadia. Ash stays on Earth to help the Nexus-8s.

An unofficial fan synopsis. Blade Runner is owned by Alcon Entertainment; the comics are published by Titan Comics.

停電
2022

Black Out 2022

Anime short · 2017Shinichiro WatanabeAbout 15 minutes

The event every later story hangs on. Three years after the first film, Tyrell's Nexus-8 replicants have open-ended lifespans, and a human supremacist movement is hunting them down. Two replicants and a human insider set out to make sure no one can find them again.

—THE BLACKOUT

The night the records died

Human thugs attack the replicant Trixie, and she is rescued by Iggy, a replicant soldier who deserted when he realised the enemy he was fighting were replicants too. He brings her into an underground movement that plans to destroy Tyrell's database of registered replicants. Trixie befriends Ren, a human technician in charge of missile launches.

Ren redirects a test missile to detonate over Los Angeles. The pulse blacks out the city and wipes its electronic records, while Iggy and Trixie drive a fuel truck into Tyrell's servers to finish the job. Trixie is killed by security. Iggy escapes and removes his right eye, the only thing that could identify him.

The fallout shapes the next twenty years. The records of every living replicant are gone, replicant production is banned, and Tyrell goes bankrupt. It takes over a decade for Wallace to win approval for a new line.

二〇二九
2029

Blade Runner 2029

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Reunion · Echoes · RedemptionMichael Green · Mike Johnson · Andres Guinaldo

Ash has been back with the LAPD since 2027, retiring replicants on paper while secretly smuggling them out. Her lover Freysa leads the Replicant Underground. Then a Nexus-6 who escaped Ash twelve years ago turns up alive, long past his four years.

Vol. 1 · Reunion

#01THE SEA WALL

Yotun redeems

An anonymous tip sends Ash to the Sepulveda Sea Wall construction site, where replicants are said to be working. One runs. When she catches him, he says "Yotun redeems" and jumps to his death. The name nags at her, and the records confirm it: Yotun, a Nexus-6 who overpowered her and escaped in 2017.

#02THE DIAMANT

The tanks

A replicant servant, Ducasse, poisons her owners Hyman and Genevieve Bask and takes one of Hyman's eyes as a "key to redemption". The jumper's eye has no serial number, so a colleague wonders if he was even a replicant. At the Diamant, a secret replicant club, Ash's question about Yotun draws the attention of a woman named Lelia, who throws a cab door at her and escapes. With Freysa, Ash follows an address to a high-rise full of glass tanks holding replicants.

#03THE SACRIFICE

A life for a life

Yotun and his lieutenant Kalia look over the tanks. Tyrell, he says, profited from making life, while he restores it. A Blade Runner named Ruskin is found with his spine torn out, and Ash inherits the Bask case. At a ceremony, a follower gives up their life to extend Yotun's. Lelia knocks Ash out at her own home and takes her away just as Freysa arrives. Yotun's group then boards an airship party for the city's elite, using Bask's eye to get in.

#04REUNION
TURNING POINT

The wall comes down

Yotun takes the airship and lectures Mayor Chen and the guests on their hypocrisy. Replicant hands built the sea wall, and he demands it. Then he blows charges planted along it. Ash wakes strapped to a chair. Jac, a replicant she once saved who now follows Yotun, frees her and shows her that Yotun is reviving Nexus-8s that were never shipped. Lelia kills Jac before he can explain more. Yotun's people leave and destroy the airship.

Vol. 2 · Echoes

#05THE DUMP

The faces she retired

Yotun hijacks a police spinner on the way to his base. Ash gets away from Kalia and Lelia but gets lost in a vast trash dump. Yotun finds her and puts her down by attacking her damaged spine. Freysa searches with Dyson and meets Silka, a new escapee who says she knows where Ash is. At the base, Ash meets replicants she once retired, brought back. Calling it mercy, Yotun lowers her into a tank and promises her a new life.

#06THE PLOY

Freysa as bait

The Underground cuts power to five sectors. Silka, Dyson and Farhad walk into Yotun's base with Freysa as a "hostage" to prove they've switched sides. Yotun suspects a trick, plays along, and takes Freysa with him. In the blackout the others reach the tanks and pull Ash out. Her spine now works perfectly. Silka is killed in the escape. Ash, who overheard Yotun's plans, knows where he's going: LAPD headquarters.

#07REBORN

No pain

Ash has no pain and her eyesight is restored. She goes after Freysa alone. Yotun's forces storm LAPD headquarters with Freysa in tow. She calls it a suicide mission. He calls it Eldon Tyrell's legacy, and reminds her that he once carried her out of an explosion at the Battle of Mesa Echo Erebus.

#08THE BROADCAST
TURNING POINT

Blood

Yotun cries out in pain, and his followers give him their blood. That is how he has outlived his expiry. He broadcasts his takeover of the city. Freysa kills the signal, and he tears out her right eye. Ash fights her way in on a motorcycle as the building is bombed. Yotun slips away, and the Underground pulls Ash and Freysa out as headquarters burns.

Vol. 3 · Redemption

#09SIX MONTHS LATER

Under suspicion

A nurse, Ambrose, fails a Voight-Kampff test and flees. Ash and Freysa protect him from Yotun's follower Pekka, and Freysa notices a dead attacker's skin is badly wrinkled. Ash keeps her leads from her sergeant, Esper, who threatens to partner her with a Blade Runner called Marlowe, then sets Marlowe on her quietly. Yotun, now in a wheelchair, welcomes Ambrose to his new hideout.

#10THE DIARY

Alice Leopold's vault

Yotun drains Ambrose's blood and leaves him catatonic. Ash answers a break-in at a vault, finds Pekka hiding in a clock tower, and watches Marlowe retire him before he can talk. The vault belonged to Alice Leopold. From it, Kalia brings Yotun a diary he wrote as a child. He says it was kept safe for him by his older brother: Eldon Tyrell.

#11THE SPINNER PLANT

Exposed

Yotun's blood cure is failing, and his followers are ageing to death. At a spinner plant, Ash finds three of them, the last whispering "Yotun redeems". Kalia calls with a farewell and bombs the spinner Ash is in. She survives. Marlowe corners Freysa at home and warns her it's too late. When Ash walks into the precinct to report where Yotun is, Esper takes her gun and badge and arrests her for working with the Underground.

#12EDENS
FINALE

The owl in the box

Ash's old captain, Lydia Wojciech, makes a scene to distract the guards, and Freysa breaks Ash out. At Yotun's hideout they find Kalia dying. She says Yotun has gone home. That means Edens, the town where Eldon Tyrell grew up. Yotun shoots their spinner down. Ash's spine starts to fail as the tank's effect wears off, and he drags her to the spot where young Tyrell buried a box. Inside are the bones of an owl, his answer to whether death can be beaten.

Ash draws on Yotun, and he yields, hoping she'll live to see others finish his work. Freysa shoots him in the head, then lifts Ash up and tells her she has a story to tell her about a miracle.

黒蓮
2032

Black Lotus

Anime series · 13 episodes · 2021Kenji Kamiyama · Shinji AramakiJessica Henwick as Elle

Ten years after the Blackout, a replicant wakes with no memory, a black lotus tattoo and a locked data device. Remembering what was done to her means tracing it back to the top of the Wallace Corporation.

E01CITY OF ANGELS

Elle

October 2032. A young woman, Elle, wakes in a transport with no memories. When a gang attacks her, she fights like a machine and remembers her name. A black-market dealer, Doc Badger, shelters her but can't crack her device, and sends her upstairs to Joseph, who is passed out drunk.

E02ALL WE ARE NOT

The senator

Joseph decrypts the device. It shows Senator Bannister meeting Niander Wallace Sr. Elle recognises Bannister as one of the men who hunted her in the desert. She confronts him at a boxing arena and drops him from a balcony. Joseph shoots her to stop her.

E03THE HUMAN CONDITION

The test

Elle wakes in Joseph's apartment. He checks her eye for a serial number and gives her a Voight-Kampff test. It says she's human. More footage shows Bannister and the journalist Hooper hunting replicants for sport.

E04THE DOLL HUNT
TURNING POINT

Dolls

At the desert site, Elle relives it. The hunters, including Hooper, Police Chief Earl Grant and his wife Josephine, call the replicants "dolls" and open fire. Elle escaped with a girl called Miu, whom Hooper later killed in an experiment. Elle stabs Hooper to death, her memories flood back, and she swears revenge on every hunter.

E05PRESSURE

Black Lotus

Elle and Joseph plan to work through the list. Chief Grant orders the elimination of the killer the press calls "Black Lotus", while a rookie officer, Alani Davis, suspects she's a replicant. Police raid Doc's shop and find only Joseph, who set it up as a trap.

E06THE PERSISTENCE OF MEMORY

The Grants

Elle breaks into the Grants' home. Josephine says she only watched and that a Doctor M organised the hunt. Grant attacks. Elle stabs him, and he accidentally shoots his wife as he dies. The Blade Runner Marlowe arrives, and Elle blows him out of the building with a gas explosion.

E07REALITY

False memories

Joseph treats Elle's wounds and hides her from Davis. Wallace Sr. prepares to sell off Tyrell's assets and bury the evidence. Doctor M runs Elle's memories through a machine and shows they were implanted and edited. She learns Wallace Sr. ran the hunt, and kills M.

E08THE DAVIS REPORT

Nobody believes her

An investigator interrogates Davis about the deaths of Bannister and Grant. She says she believes replicant production has secretly restarted. He finds replicant remains in the Nevada desert, but Davis is put on leave for "delusions".

E09FREE WILL

The penthouse

Elle infiltrates the Wallace Corporation to kill Wallace Sr., while Joseph snipes from outside and wounds Marlowe. Security seals Wallace in his penthouse. Elle reaches him anyway. He shoots her, and she kills him with his own gun, then jumps from the window into Joseph's arms.

E10CLAIR DE LUNE

The boyfriend

Joseph remembers Selene, a singer he loved, whom Marlowe made him kill. Elle wonders why the man she loved never came looking. With a memory device she sketches his face. Joseph recognises him: Niander Wallace Jr., who has been using Joseph all along.

E11ALL THE BEST MEMORIES

Built to kill

Wallace Jr. orders Joseph to retire Elle. Joseph tells her the truth instead: Jr. made her to kill his father. She agrees to have her memories erased, and Joseph reports her dead. Jr. sends another replicant assassin, Water Lily, anyway, and Davis is alerted to protect Elle.

E12ARTIFICIAL SOULS

Water Lily

Elle fights Water Lily until police arrive, then escapes. Water Lily impales Davis before she can report Jr. Joseph kills Marlowe, Selene's real killer, and collapses. Elle vows to kill Wallace Jr.

E13TIME TO DIE
FINALE

The blind maker

Elle confronts Wallace Jr. in Tyrell's old building. Her programming won't let her attack him. She fights Water Lily, whom Jr. kills himself. He offers Elle a place at his side, and she slashes his eyes. Joseph seals himself in the replicant factory and blows it up to save her.

Months later, Davis is back at work in a wheelchair. The now-blind Wallace Jr., the Niander Wallace of 2049, plans to replace humanity with his replicants. Elle rides Joseph's motorbike out of Los Angeles into the desert.

Comic · Black Lotus: Leaving L.A. · 4 issues · 2022 · Nancy A. Collins · Enid Balam

#01FRACKTOWN

The desert

Scavengers ambush Elle on a desert road. She beats them and takes their truck to haul her broken spinner bike to Fracktown, a mining town. Boss Barnes, who owns the fracking operation, demands to buy her bike. Miguel, leader of the nearby Inland Empire clean-energy co-op, defuses the standoff and offers her a place to rest. Barnes has a corporate repo man waiting to talk about broken contracts, after the replicant workers at the town's brothel started turning up dead.

#02THE CO-OP

Hostages

At a dinner in Elle's honour, a sniper hits Miguel and raiders storm the co-op, taking hostages including his wife Nyoko and daughter Kaja. Miguel, a former Blade Runner, has known all along that Elle is a replicant. He asks her to bring his family home and gives her a family heirloom, a katana.

#03THE FRACKTORY

One-woman assault

Barnes calls the co-op's armed defence a "hostile takeover" and demands it disarm. Miguel stalls with talk of a council vote while Elle hits Barnes's fracking plant alone, ending in an explosive rescue.

#04THE SHOWDOWN
FINALE

Kozlov

Nyoko and Kaja come home. Preston, a survivor of an ambush on the freed workers, confesses his love for Nyoko and admits he worked with Barnes to get Miguel out of the way. Kozlov, Fracktown's security chief, takes over his cousin's operation and tells the town the co-op are eco-terrorists who blew up the plant. It ends with Elle facing Kozlov in a showdown in Fracktown, and with a choice about whether to kill.

Comic · Black Lotus: Las Vegas · 4 issues · 2025–26 · Nancy A. Collins · Jesús Hervás

#01THE DOLL SQUAD

Paradise

Blind and obsessed, Niander Wallace Jr. builds a hunting team, the "Doll Squad", under his operative Menzes. It includes Davis, promised she'll walk again and get off-world with her wife; Kozlov, out for revenge for his cousin; and Kate Marlowe, sister of the Blade Runner Elle killed. Starving in the Mojave, Elle raids a convoy, passes the ruins of the Death Valley Spa where early memories stir, and reaches the irradiated ruins of Las Vegas. Someone there welcomes her to "Paradise".

#02PAX VEGAS

The court of machines

The Paradise casino is a replicant kingdom run by Cleopatra and Nefertiti, survivors of the bomb who took the city from rival factions. The self-styled emperors Julius, Caligula and Nero decide Elle must be human, since she has no serial code. Facing execution, she reveals she's a next-generation assassin built by Wallace. The Doll Squad is ambushed by mercenaries on the way in, and the survivors are captured by Caesar's soldiers.

#03THE ARENA

A princess of Vegas

The queens dress Elle as a princess who must marry one of the three kings and can never leave. A human slave, Vanna, tells her how her brother was sent to the arena. At a gladiator show in Elle's honour, humans fight in bomb collars. Davis turns up trying to protect her, and Elle refuses to watch any more killing and attacks the creature set loose. A stranger pulls her out as Julius sentences her to death.

#04THE UNDERBELLY
FINALE

Viva las replicantes

Nefertiti stabs Julius, and Paradise falls into civil war. Vanna leads Elle and Davis to the tunnels under the casinos, held by the Semita, technology-worshipping replicants. Underground, Davis admits who she works for, and Elle tells her how Wallace Jr. groomed her, made her kill his father and then sent Water Lily to erase her. Topside, Menzes's team is captured as Paradise burns. Elle finds the Semita, gets back her battered bike, and is welcomed with a cheer. The story is set up to continue.

夜明け
2036

2036: Nexus Dawn

Short film · 2017Luke ScottJared Leto as Niander Wallace

Niander Wallace comes before a panel of lawmakers to get the replicant ban lifted, with a new model at his side.

—THE HEARING

Will you die for me?

Wallace argues that Earth's collapsing ecosystem can only be kept alive with cheap replicant labour, and that his new replicants are no danger to anyone. The lawmakers aren't convinced. So he turns to his assistant, a Nexus-9. He orders it to cut itself, then makes it choose between his life and its own. It cuts its own throat in front of them. Wallace asks again whether they will approve production.

The answer comes later in the timeline: the ban is lifted, and Wallace's obedient Nexus-9s go into production.

二〇三九
2039

Blade Runner 2039

Comic · 12 issuesVols. Luv · Upgrade · AshMichael Green · Mike Johnson · Andres Guinaldo

Wallace has given the LAPD its first replicant Blade Runner: Luv. Ash, now ageing and failing, runs the Underground's escape routes with Freysa. Wallace wants Alexander Selwyn's research on replicant reproduction, and Isobel Selwyn holds the key.

Vol. 1 · Luv

#01LUV

The first replicant Blade Runner

In a food court, Luv confronts a young replicant, Tabitha, chases her into a meat locker and snaps her neck while her friend Lexi watches. At headquarters Luv's numbers are the best in the unit, and her human colleagues hate her for it. Wallace praises her and sets a new task: find the replicant copy of Selwyn's wife. Lexi buys new eyes, finds the boatman known as the Ferryman, and reaches Catalina, where Ash is waiting and asks for the eyes.

#02SANTA BARBARA

New eyes

At the camp Ash finds Freysa with Sapper Morton, who served with her off-world. The eyes Lexi brought go to Freysa, who lost one to Yotun, and to Ash, whose eyes, spine and lungs are failing. At Selwyn's old mansion, Luv meets the butler Clarence and the owner, Penelope, who turns out to be an older replicant. She beats Luv. Clarence calls her by her real name: Hythe. Meanwhile, Cleo Selwyn is back on Earth.

#03THE DISK

Cleo returns

Ash visits the elderly Lydia Wojciech in her care home, who warns her to stay away from Luv and from Wallace. At a noodle bar, Cleo finds Ash and tells her Isobel has vanished. Hythe hands Ash a disk of Selwyn's research before gunmen open fire. Luv calls Cleo's hotel room, gun drawn.

#04THE BLUFF

An empty disk

Twin hackers decrypt the disk. It shows Selwyn's team made several Isobels and came close to replicant fertility. Luv confronts Cleo. Ash bets that Luv won't kill humans, because that would end the replicant Blade Runner experiment, and hands Luv a disk she says is the research. It's blank. Ash and Cleo escape over the Sea Wall. Freysa wants the research destroyed. Cleo guesses Isobel went to San Francisco. Wallace gives Luv a new partner: a replicant copy of Ash.

Vol. 2 · Upgrade

#05RASH

Ash's double

Wallace introduces Rash, built from Ash's DNA, obedient in everything except killing humans. Ash and Freysa fight over Selwyn's research: Ash wants to use it, Freysa calls it obscene. Ash and Cleo drive north for San Francisco with Lexi hidden in the back. Raiders attack the truck. Lexi shoots one dead, and Cleo is wounded.

#06THE FARMER

Rash's first retirement

A farmer takes in Ash, Cleo and Lexi. Luv and Rash work their way through Ash's contacts. Hythe ambushes them and is shot dead by Rash, her first retirement. As Ash leaves for San Francisco, the farmer holds her at gunpoint: he's a Nexus-6 she once hunted.

#07MACK

The one who got away

In 2018 Ash shot the replicant Mack off a rooftop and told local kids where to find his parts. He survived. Now Lexi's presence convinces him Ash has changed, and he joins her. In the quake-emptied city of San Francisco, they search the house of Isobel's relatives, where Luv and Rash are waiting. Rash shoots Mack.

#08ISOBEL
TURNING POINT

Isobel returns

Rash breaks one of Ash's fingers, then one of her own, out of curiosity. Isobel hijacks the Blade Runners' spinner and crashes it into the house. Mack dies covering Ash, and Luv falls into the sea. At the farm Isobel explains that she led Wallace's people away from Cleo. Ash decides the only way out is to kill Wallace, not knowing he is listening.

Vol. 3 · Ash

#09GAFF

An origami frog

Wojciech has died. Gaff tells Ash that to get close to Wallace she must bring him what he wants, and leaves her an origami frog. Rash follows Ash into a trap, fights Sapper and is tranquilised. Under questioning she says Isobel is the missing piece of Wallace's fertility work. The LAPD puts Luv's contract under review. Ash goes to a club to find someone with a way in: Cal Moreaux, the first Blade Runner.

#10THE FAVOUR

Cal Moreaux

Cal warns Ash that Wallace rarely leaves his headquarters, but he owes her a favour and agrees to help. At the hideout, Rash breaks free and takes Isobel hostage, then asks to join them. When Luv arrives and orders her to hand Isobel over, Rash knocks her out and asks again.

#11DAISY

The nursemaid

Cal points Ash to Daisy, Wallace's old nursemaid and supplier, who sets her pleasure model on them. Freysa questions the captive Luv and decides she's in love with her maker. Wallace comes to the meeting Daisy set up, expecting Ash. He has studied her illness and says he can cure it.

#12ASH
FINALE

What Wallace doesn't see

Rash, disguised as Ash, turns down Wallace's offer and fights off his guards. The real Ash waits in his spinner and takes him to Eldon Tyrell's old sanctum, where Tyrell died twenty years earlier. She refuses the cure and gives Wallace Selwyn's research in exchange for leaving Cleo and Isobel alone. She hopes he cracks replicant birth and never perfects their obedience.

Cleo and Isobel leave for the colonies, with Rash to protect them. Ash keeps the cure, and how little time she has, to herself, and goes home to spend it with Freysa.

逃げ場なし
2048

2048: Nowhere to Run

Short film · 2017Luke ScottDave Bautista as Sapper Morton

Sapper Morton, an old Nexus-8 combat model, lives quietly as a farmer outside Los Angeles. One trip into the city ends his hiding.

—THE MARKET

Ella

In the city, Sapper stops to talk with a girl, Ella, and gives her a book he likes, The Power and the Glory. He sells his harvest at the market, still short of the money he needs, and walks past a gang of thugs who try to provoke him.

Later the same gang turns on Ella and her mother. Sapper steps in and beats them with a soldier's strength, killing most of them. Shaken by what he has done, he leaves, dropping his identity papers. A bystander who saw him earlier calls the LAPD to report a rogue replicant. That call is what sends K to his farm at the start of 2049.

二〇四九
2049

Blade Runner 2049

Film · 2017Denis VilleneuveRyan Gosling · Harrison Ford · Ana de Armas · Sylvia Hoeks

Thirty years after the original, a new-model replicant Blade Runner finds proof of something that should be impossible: a replicant who gave birth.

ITHE TREE

A box under a dead tree

K, a Nexus-9 Blade Runner, retires Sapper Morton at his farm. Under a dead tree he finds a buried box holding the bones of a replicant woman who died after giving birth by caesarean section. His superior, Lt. Joshi, sees a world-breaking secret and orders every trace of it, including the child, destroyed. K goes home to Joi, his holographic companion.

IITHE HORSE

A memory that might be real

At the Wallace Corporation, archives identify the bones as an experimental Nexus-7: Rachael, who vanished with Deckard thirty years earlier. Niander Wallace wants the child, the key to replicants who can breed, and sends his enforcer Luv to follow K. A date carved into the tree matches a childhood memory of K's: a wooden horse he hid in a furnace. At an orphanage in the wastelands he finds the horse in the furnace, exactly where he remembered hiding it.

The memory designer Dr. Ana Stelline tells him the memory is real, someone lived it. K begins to believe he's the child. He fails his baseline test badly. Joshi gives him 48 hours to pass it, or be retired himself. Luv kills Joshi.

IIILAS VEGAS
TURNING POINT

Deckard

Traces of radiation on the horse lead K to the ruins of Las Vegas and Rick Deckard, hiding there for thirty years. Luv attacks, destroys Joi and takes Deckard to Wallace, who offers him a copy of Rachael in exchange for the child. Deckard refuses, and Luv kills the copy. Rescued by Freysa's replicant resistance, K learns the truth: the child was a girl. He isn't special. His memory was hers.

IVSNOW
FINALE

The daughter

Freysa wants Deckard dead before he can lead Wallace to his daughter. K intercepts Luv's convoy at the sea wall instead, and drowns her in the surf. Badly wounded, he brings Deckard to Dr. Stelline's lab, the woman who makes memories and who is Deckard and Rachael's daughter. Deckard goes in to meet her, while K lies down on the steps in the falling snow.
